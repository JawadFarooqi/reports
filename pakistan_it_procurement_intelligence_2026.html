Pakistan Fiscal Calendar
Government fiscal year runs July–June. Budget is approved in June; most procurement plans are released July–September (Q1). Q2 (Oct–Dec) sees the highest volume of RFP publications as funds are released. Q3 (Jan–Mar) is mid-cycle cleanup. Q4 (Apr–Jun) is procurement freeze as fiscal year closes — very few new tenders, focus on renewals. Best time to pitch: Sep–Nov.

Section 04 — Vendor Registration & Bid Winning

How to Qualify & Win

The two most common failure points in Pakistani IT procurement: (1) being disqualified before evaluation because of a missing document, and (2) losing on technical score because the scoring criteria weren't understood before writing the proposal. This section addresses both.

Bid submitted after deadline by even one minute (EPADS timestamps electronically)

Missing mandatory documents listed in SBD Section 2 (varies per tender)
Bidding document fee not paid or wrong amount (check exact fee on each NIT)
Conflict of interest — firm worked on specs/TOR for same tender (common trap in consulting)

Technical score below minimum qualifying threshold (usually 70/100) before financial envelope opened
Joint venture partners not all listed and individually compliant

National Competitive Bidding (NCB)
Open to all eligible vendors. Minimum 21 days public notice. Must be published on PPRA website AND in at least one national newspaper simultaneously. Most IT software contracts fall in this range.

International Competitive Bidding (ICB)
Must be published internationally. English language mandatory. 45 days minimum notice. Foreign firms can participate directly. Large ERP, core banking, and national platform contracts sit here.

Section 06 — Closed Market Navigation

Private Sector Entry Map

Pakistan's private banks, telecoms, and large corporates do not publish open tenders. Their procurement is relationship-driven, panel-based, and accessed through specific registration pathways. This section documents how to get inside those closed systems — because the contract opportunity is there, the door is just unmarked.
